📖 THE MODULE PROJECT
Over this module you will read seven traditions — the Bhagavad Gita, the Bible, the Confucian Analects, Buddhist parables, the Qur’an, Plato, and Aristotle — and each one offers an answer to a single question: what does it mean to live well, and what endures? At the end of the module you will pull those answers together into a project: an essay in which you weigh them and stake out your own view, and a VR experience in which a visitor can meet the thinkers you studied. You do not write all of that at the end. You build it a little each week, starting today.

📝 TODAY'S WORK · THREE STEPS
1
Submit your Quick Write. Finish Arjuna’s Letter and upload the Word file to the Quick Write assignment in Canvas. Then save a copy in your Module 1 folder if it is not there already.
2
Open your project document (M1_HowToLiveWell) and add a heading: The Bhagavad Gita. Under it, write three or four sentences summarizing the Gita’s answer to how a person should live — the deathless self, acting without attachment to results, and the steady, self-governed person. Use your Learning Log entries from this week.
3
Connect it to a bigger question. Still in the project document, answer one of these in a few sentences:
📝 THE BIG QUESTIONS · ON THE PAGE
Where do I come from?
Why am I here?
What can I hope for?
What do I owe others?
What is my purpose?
Which question does the Gita speak to most directly, and do you find its answer convincing? You will return to these questions with every text.

Check that three things are done: the Quick Write is submitted in Canvas, your project document has a Bhagavad Gita section, and you have written a few sentences connecting the Gita to one of the big questions. That is one tradition recorded. Six to go.
