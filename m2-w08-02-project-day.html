Lesson 8.2 · Project Day
Trade Feedback, Then Revise
Friday · a fresh reader, the rubric, and your biggest fixes
📝 TODAY'S WORK · THREE STEPS
1
Share & receive. Trade projects with a partner (or small group). Each reviewer, using the rubric, gives the maker: what the interpretation seems to be, the one spot they got lost, and one real question.
2
Decide what to act on. You don’t have to take every note — but if a reader missed your point, that’s usually a sign to fix it, not to explain it away.
3
Revise. Spend the rest of the session on your biggest fix — an argument-level change first (Level 1), not a surface tweak.
⭐ ALSO ON FRIDAYS · SENIOR STUDIOS (~30 MIN)
Your pitch deck comes due this Friday: use Save As to create your _Final file, check it against the success criteria on the Pitch Deck Draft assignment, and submit it in Canvas. Today’s skill transfers directly: get a real listener to react to one slide the way a peer just reacted to your project. Watching someone meet your work fresh tells you more than any amount of polishing alone.
✅ BEFORE YOU LOG OFF

Check three things: you gave a partner specific, useful feedback, you captured the notes on your own project, and you’ve made at least one real revision today. Next week: final polish and submit.
