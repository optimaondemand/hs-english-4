Friday · the last polish, the final check, the hand-in
📖 WHERE YOU ARE
This is it — the project you chose in Week 3 and built across seven weeks comes in today. The thinking is done; this session is for the final polish (Level 3) and a clean submission. Make it the best version of what you made.
📝 TODAY'S WORK · THREE STEPS
1
Final polish. Last surface pass — conventions, concision, audio/video/visual quality, file names. Read or play it through once more, clean.
2
Rubric check. Run down the core rubric and your format’s craft row one last time. Anything still in “Proficient” you can lift to “Excellent” in the time you have?
3
Submit. Turn it in to the Module 2 Project assignment in the right format and file name, and save a copy in your Module 2 folder. Then complete the closing reflection.
⭐ ALSO ON FRIDAYS · SENIOR STUDIOS (~30 MIN)
Your pitch deck is submitted, so the Studio rests while you ship the module project. The finishing discipline you just used — polish, check against a standard, ship — is exactly what Quarter 3 asks, when the deck meets your voice. With spare minutes, place your deck and your Quarter 1 brief together in your Senior Studios folder; they are the first two artifacts of your Portfolio.
✅ BEFORE YOU LOG OFF

Three things: your project is submitted in the correct format and file name, a copy is saved in your Module 2 folder, and you’ve done the closing reflection. That completes Module 2 — nicely done.
