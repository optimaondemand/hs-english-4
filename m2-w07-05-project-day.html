Get It Whole — and Read the Rubric
Friday · finish the build, then check it against how it will be graded
📖 WHERE YOU ARE
This is the last build week before the two-week finale of revising and submitting. By the end of this week your project should be whole — complete start to finish — with the Nietzsche layer folded in where it fits. Today you push it across the finish line for a first complete version, then measure it against the rubric you’ll actually be graded on.
📝 TODAY'S WORK · THREE STEPS
1
Finish the build. Using your two VR work sessions this week, get every part of the project drafted or built — no gaps left.
2
Open the rubric. Find the Module 2 Project assignment and read the core rubric and your format’s craft row. Score your own draft honestly against each criterion.
3
List your top three fixes. Where does your draft fall short of “Excellent”? Those become your revision plan for next week.
⭐ ALSO ON FRIDAYS · SENIOR STUDIOS (~30 MIN)
Skeptic pass on your deck: run each slide past Claude with a narrow prompt (react as a skeptical investor; do not rewrite), ask for cut-word lists, and make every change yourself. Log the sessions, then walk the deck once in your VR space. Nietzsche carries over: values are argued for, not just assumed. Don’t assume your app is good — make the case for why it’s good and for whom, the way you’d defend a claim.
✅ BEFORE YOU LOG OFF

Check three things: your project is whole, you’ve self-scored it against the rubric, and you have a top-three fix list for revision. Also submit this week’s Quick Write. Next week is all revision.
