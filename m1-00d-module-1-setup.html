Module Setup
Build Your Learning Folder
Do this once, before Lesson 1.1. It takes about ten minutes.
📖 WHY YOU'RE DOING THIS
This module is organized around one question: What does it mean to live well, and what in a human life endures? You will read seven traditions, each with its own answer. To keep your notes and your project work in one place, you will use a folder in OneDrive instead of a paper notebook. Set it up now; you will use it every day this module.
📝 SET IT UP · FOUR STEPS
1
Sign in to Microsoft 365 with your Optima account and open OneDrive. Create a new folder named exactly:
English IV — Module 1 (Ancient Wisdom)
2
Inside that folder, create a Word document named M1_LearningLog_LastName.docx. This is your Learning Log — the home for every warm-up, note, and reflection a lesson asks you to write. (This replaces a notebook. When a lesson says “write it in your Learning Log,” this is the file it means.)
3
Create a second Word document named M1_HowToLiveWell_LastName.docx. This is your Project Workspace. Each week you will add a short section about the tradition you studied; by the end of the module these become your culminating essay. Your weekly Quick Writes feed straight into this file.
4
Back at the top level of OneDrive, create a second folder named exactly:
English IV — Senior Studios
Inside it, create a Word document named ENG4_SeniorStudios_LastName.docx. This is your Studio Notebook: it lives all year and holds your app concept in one line, your audience research, your Friday Studio work, and your pitch drafts. Your pitch deck will live in this folder too. (Read the Senior Studios Briefing, setup step 2, if you have not yet.)
📁 WHAT YOUR FOLDER SHOULD LOOK LIKE
📁 OneDrive
  ├─ 📁 English IV — Module 1 (Ancient Wisdom)
  │    ├─ 📄 M1_LearningLog_LastName.docx  ← daily notes & reflections
  │    └─ 📄 M1_HowToLiveWell_LastName.docx  ← your project, built week by week
  └─ 📁 English IV — Senior Studios
      └─ 📄 ENG4_SeniorStudios_LastName.docx  ← your Studio Notebook, all year
Replace LastName with your own last name. Your VR snapshots, Quick Writes, and later your pitch deck will be saved in these folders as the year goes on.
✏️ PREP YOUR LEARNING LOG
At the top of M1_LearningLog, paste these three headings so the file is ready to fill in as you go:
Module 1 — The Big Question: What does it mean to live well, and what endures?
1. Lesson Notes & Reflections — label each entry by lesson (e.g., “Lesson 1.1”)
2. Vocabulary Log — a running table: word | what it means | the sentence I met it in
3. My Answers, So Far — where you record how your own thinking changes across the module
4. AI-Use Log — what I asked Claude | what it gave me | what I kept | what was wrong (see Working with Claude, part 2)
💡 HOW EACH WEEK WORKS
Three literature lessons (close reading of the week’s text), one grammar & vocabulary lesson, and a Friday Project Day. Each week has one Quick Write (assigned Monday, due Friday) and two short VR activities. Fridays also give your Senior Studios pitch about thirty minutes, and once this quarter you will record a short recitation of a passage you have studied. Keep all of this work in your Module 1 folder; at the end of the module you will use it to write an essay on how a person should live.
✅ SHOW YOU'RE SET UP
Take a screenshot of OneDrive showing both folders (Module 1 with its two Word files, and Senior Studios with your Studio Notebook) and upload it to the Setup Check assignment in Canvas, along with the two short confirmations it asks for. That is the only thing you submit today — it tells your teacher your workspace and your Claude account are ready.
