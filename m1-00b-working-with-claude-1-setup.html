Getting Claude Running for This Course
Nothing here is graded. Read it once now, then come back to it whenever you set up new work.
🎯 THE ONE RULE, BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE
Claude is an assistant, not an author. In this course you use it to help your thinking: to check a fact, sort your notes, pressure-test a plan, or research an audience for your pitch. You do not use it to do your reading, your interpreting, your drafting, or your word choices. Those are the actual work of an English student, and they are what your grade rewards. Every place an assignment invites you to use Claude, it also asks you to log what you did, so the line between your work and its help stays clear.
🤖 WHAT CLAUDE IS, AND WHERE IT LIVES
Claude is an AI assistant you talk to in plain language. As an Optima senior you have your own Optima-issued Claude account, and Claude runs in its own separate app: it is not inside Canvas, not inside Word, and not the same as any other assistant you may have used. You will use it across your senior courses, so setting it up carefully now pays off all year.
Claude can also work with the actual files in a folder you point it at. That matters, because your work in this course lives in real files: a Word draft, your Learning Log, an outline, a set of notes. Pointed at your course folder, Claude can look at those files with you and help you work on them, instead of you copying and pasting text back and forth.
You sign in with your own account. Your conversations and files are yours; do not use anyone else’s login, do not share yours, and do not paste personal information (yours or anyone’s) into a conversation.
⚖️ WHAT IT CAN AND CANNOT DO FOR YOU
✅ GOOD USES (IT HELPS)
  • Explain a hard sentence or an unfamiliar word
  • Check a date or historical fact you then verify
  • Sort or reorganize notes you already wrote
  • Ask “what am I missing?” about your own outline
  • Research who your pitch audience is and what they value
  • Turn your rough bullet points into a cleaner list to react to
⛔ NOT FOR THIS COURSE (IT REPLACES YOU)
  • Writing your essay, Quick Write, or pitch script
  • Reading the Gita or Frankenstein so you do not have to
  • Producing your interpretation or your thesis
  • Choosing your words, images, or phrasing
  • Handing you “quotes” you paste in without checking
A good test: if the tool did the thinking, it was the wrong use. If it helped you do your thinking, it was the right one.
🔧 GET IT RUNNING · FOUR STEPS
Exact setup steps depend on your device, so follow the current official setup guide and ask your facilitator if a step does not match your screen. The shape is always the same:
1
Sign in with your own account. Use the Claude account Optima set up for you. Keep the password somewhere safe.
2
Install the Claude app and open it. It is its own application, separate from Canvas and Word. You type what you want in plain language; there is no complicated menu to learn.
3
Point it at your course folder. Open it in your English IV folder in OneDrive so it can see the files you are working on. Now it can read your draft or your notes when you ask.
4
Try a first, safe request. Ask something low-stakes, such as: List the files in this folder or Explain what a synthesis essay is in two sentences. You are just confirming it works and getting used to talking to it.
