Lesson 8.1 · Revision Workshop
Revise Like It Matters
Turning a whole rough project into a strong finished one — in any format
📅 THESE LAST TWO WEEKS
No new reading — these two weeks are for finishing your module project. This week you revise (this workshop + a peer-feedback Project Day + your VR work time); next week you polish and submit. Keep everything in your Module 2 folder.
🎯 OBJECTIVE
By the end of this lesson you will be able to revise your project at three levels — argument, structure, and surface — using the rubric and a partner’s feedback, whatever format you chose.
📚 REVISION IS NOT JUST POLISHING

It’s tempting, this late, to only fix surface things — a typo, a clumsy line, a rough edit. But the biggest gains come from re-seeing the project: is the interpretation actually clear and arguable? Does every part serve it? Is the strongest evidence in the strongest place? Save the surface polish for last. First, make the thinking better — that’s what the top three rubric rows (interpretation, evidence, depth = 70 of 100 points) reward.

📋 A THREE-LEVEL REVISION CHECKLIST · ANY FORMAT
Level 1 — The argument
☐ Is your interpretation clear in the first minute (or first lines/scene)?
☐ Does every section, scene, entry, or beat serve that one point? Cut or fix what wanders.
☐ Did you engage a real complication or counter-view (the “Depth” row)?
Level 2 — The structure & evidence
☐ Is your strongest evidence in the strongest spot — not buried?
☐ Is each quotation or textual moment actually explained, not just dropped in?
☐ Does the order build? Would moving one piece make it land harder?
Level 3 — The surface (last)
☐ Conventions clean — commas, quotations, the grammar from this module?
☐ Concise — cut the dead words (Lesson 6.4)?
☐ Format details right — audio clear, captions legible, file named correctly?
✅ REVISION OR POLISH?
⚡ SORT EACH CHANGE
Is each a deep revision (the thinking) or surface polish? Do the deep ones first.
1. Rewriting your opening so your interpretation is clear immediately.
2. Fixing the audio levels on your podcast.
3. Adding the counter-view your draft was missing.
💬 GET A SECOND SET OF EYES
On Project Day this week you’ll trade work with a partner. The best feedback is specific and kind: name what the interpretation seems to be, point to the one spot you got lost, and ask one real question. You can also use your Claude account to test your draft (“where is my argument weakest?”) — but a human reader will catch what matters most, and the revisions stay yours.
✏️ DO THIS NOW
Run Level 1 first on your project — the argument — and make the biggest fix it needs before touching anything on the surface. Then move to Level 2. Use your VR work sessions this week as your revision studio. Save Level 3 polish for next week.
➡️ YOUR NEXT STEP

Bring your project to Project Day for partner feedback, then keep revising. Next week: final polish and submission, against the rubric you’ve had in hand since Week 7.
