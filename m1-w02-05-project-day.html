📖 WHERE YOU ARE
Last week you recorded the Bhagavad Gita’s answer to how a person should live. This week you add the Bible. By the end of the module you will have seven traditions side by side in one document, ready to weigh against each other and against your own view. Today you add the second.
📝 TODAY'S GUIDING QUESTION
Where does the Bible’s view of the good life agree with the Gita’s,
and where do they differ?
📝 TODAY'S WORK · THREE STEPS
1
Submit your Quick Write. Finish the Bible Quick Write and upload the Word file to the Quick Write assignment in Canvas. Save a copy in your Module 1 folder.
2
Add to your project document. In M1_HowToLiveWell, add a heading The Bible. In three or four sentences, summarize its answer to how a person should live and what endures — the human made in God’s image and from dust, the “blessed” life of the Sermon on the Mount, and what Psalm 23 and Romans 5 say can be depended on. Use your Learning Log entries.
3
Compare and connect. Add two or three sentences answering: Where does the Bible’s view of the good life agree with the Gita’s, and where do they differ? This is the kind of comparison your final essay will be built from.
💡 ALSO THIS WEEK
In your two VR activities you add a second station — for the Bible — to the Hall of Thinkers you began last week. Keep the snapshots in your Module 1 folder.
⭐ ALSO TODAY · SENIOR STUDIOS (~30 MIN)
In your Studio Notebook, describe the specific people who have your problem. A real need is specific, has real people attached, and is unmet or badly met by what exists now. Write until a stranger could picture your users.
✅ BEFORE YOU LOG OFF

Check three things: the Quick Write is submitted, your project document now has a Bible section, and you have written a short comparison between the Bible and the Gita. Two traditions down, five to go.
