🏛️ WHAT SENIOR STUDIOS IS
Before senior year begins, every Optima senior proposes an AI-app concept to school leadership and gets it approved. That approval happens outside of any course, so no class time is spent inventing the idea or asking permission. You arrive with an approved concept and carry the same concept into whichever core courses you take. Each course turns it into a different professional artifact:
COURSE THE ARTIFACT IT OWNS
EnglishA Shark Tank-style investor pitchRhetoric, narrative, delivery
HistoryMarket & Ethics ReportResearch, responsible innovation
ScienceTechnical Design DocHow the AI works, and its limits
MathFinancial & Feasibility ModelThe numbers behind the idea
Every stream stands alone. If English is your only Studios course, you will finish a complete pitch and lose nothing. If you take several, the streams share research and your artifacts collect in one Portfolio you carry out of Optima.
🎯 WHAT YOU DELIVER IN ENGLISH
By the end of Quarter 4: a six-slide deck built in PowerPoint, a delivered pitch of about three minutes, and prepared answers to the questions a panel of investors (the “sharks”) would ask. The deck follows a standard sequence you will learn and then adapt to your own app:
SLIDE 01
The Hook
Thirty seconds that earn attention
SLIDE 02
The Problem
Who has it, how real, how widespread
SLIDE 03
The App
One plain sentence, no jargon
SLIDE 04
Why It Works
Evidence a non-specialist can follow
SLIDE 05
The Story
One user, before and after
SLIDE 06
The Ask
What you want, what they get
📅 HOW IT IS BUILT · ONE GRADED STEP PER QUARTER
You do not build a pitch in a week. Each quarter has one graded Studio assignment that feeds the final; every Friday gives it about thirty minutes, carved out of your existing course time, not added on top.
QUARTER GRADED STEP WHAT IT IS
Q1 (this quarter)Audience & Problem BriefName your investors, state the problem your app answers, and prove the problem is real. Due at the end of Module 1.
Q2Pitch Deck DraftBuild the six-slide deck in PowerPoint and map your persuasive appeals to each slide.
Q3Delivery RunRehearse and record the pitch in your VR space; self-assess pace and presence.
Q4The Final PitchDeliver the deck, the pitch, and the Q&A. Stored in your Portfolio.
This semester covers the two highlighted rows. Reading, discussion, and Friday craft work support these steps but are not extra Studio assignments.
🛠️ THE TOOLS YOU WILL USE
PowerPoint (Microsoft 365): the six-slide deck lives here.
Claude: your Optima Claude account, in a separate app, for researching your audience and pressure-testing your message. Setup steps 3 and 4 of Start Here get you running.
Your VR space: where you workshop the deck, rehearse, and (if you choose) present.
Canvas: where every Studio assignment is submitted.
📋 HOW IT IS GRADED · ONE RUNNING RUBRIC
Every Studio assignment this year is judged on the same five criteria, so the final pitch is graded on skills you have practiced all year, not on a single performance:
01  Persuasion
02  Structure
03  Audience fit
04  Delivery (from Q3 on)
05  Polish & precision
It takes courage to present to a panel, honesty to make only claims you can defend, and perseverance to revise one piece of work for a year. That is the point.
⚖️ WHERE ENGLISH ENDS AND THE OTHER STREAMS BEGIN
English grades how well the case is made: argument, story, audience, and delivery. Whether the numbers hold is Math’s stream; how the AI actually works is Science’s; the market and its ethics are History’s. If English is your only stream, make basic, good-faith claims in those areas; you are scored on persuasion, not on the accuracy of the finance. If you are in those courses too, you may reuse your own shared research, as long as each course’s submission does its own discipline’s work.
❓ IF YOU DO NOT HAVE AN APPROVED CONCEPT YET
If you enrolled mid-year or your concept has not been approved yet, message your facilitator this week. They will connect you with the approval process. In the meantime, keep up with the Friday Studio steps using a working idea; everything you build transfers once your concept is approved.
