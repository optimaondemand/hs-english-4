📖 WHERE YOU ARE
You have a full first draft from last week. 📝 TODAY'S WORK · THREE STEPS
1
Reread your draft and mark its weakest spot — the part that’s thin, unclear, or unconvincing. That’s where this week’s building goes first.
2
Find one place Nietzsche fits. Could “who decides what is good?” deepen your argument or scene? Even one paragraph, line, or beat that brings his challenge to bear will raise the level of your project.
3
Plan your VR sessions. Decide what you’ll do in each of this week’s two VR work sessions (build, storyboard, rehearse, or map — whatever your format needs).
🧐 THIS WEEK, VR IS YOUR WORKSHOP
Your two VR sessions this week are dedicated project build time. Use the headset the way your format needs it: build the experience, storyboard and stage scenes, rehearse and record a talk, or map your argument in space and talk it through aloud. The two VR activity pages walk you through it.
⭐ ALSO ON FRIDAYS · SENIOR STUDIOS (~30 MIN)
Deck time: build Slide 6 (The Ask) and write speaker notes for every slide: what you will say, and which appeal (ethos, logos, or pathos) it leans on. This week’s thinking carries straight over: Nietzsche asks who decides what is good, and your app makes its own quiet claim about what’s good and for whom. Sharpen that claim — it’s the heart of your pitch.
✅ BEFORE YOU LOG OFF

Check three things: you’ve marked your draft’s weakest spot, found one place Nietzsche deepens it, and have a plan for your two VR sessions. Also submit this week’s Quick Write. Next week is the last build week before you revise and finish.
