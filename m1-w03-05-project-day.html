📖 WHERE YOU ARE
You now have three answers to how a person should live: the Gita’s inner steadiness, the Bible’s account of dignity and the blessed life, and Confucius’s practice of virtue among others. Today you add the third — and for the first time you have enough to compare three traditions at once.
📝 THE COMPARISON YOU'RE BUILDING TOWARD
The Gita points inward to a calm self; Confucius points outward to how we treat others. Where does the Bible fall between them, and which is closest to your own view so far?
📝 TODAY'S WORK · THREE STEPS
1
Submit your Quick Write. Finish the Confucius Quick Write and upload it to the Quick Write assignment in Canvas. Save a copy in your Module 1 folder.
2
Add to your project document. In M1_HowToLiveWell, add a heading The Confucian Analects. In three or four sentences, summarize Confucius’s answer — goodness as steady practice, character over rank, and virtue lived out among others. Use your Learning Log entries.
3
Compare three traditions. Add a few sentences: The Gita points inward to a calm self; Confucius points outward to how we treat others. Where does the Bible fall between them, and which is closest to your own view so far? This three-way comparison is the backbone of your final essay.
💡 ALSO THIS WEEK
Your two VR activities add a third station — for Confucius — to the Hall of Thinkers. Keep the snapshots in your Module 1 folder.
⭐ ALSO TODAY · SENIOR STUDIOS (~30 MIN)
Evidence hunt: find one statistic, source, or concrete example showing your problem is real, and verify it with your own eyes. You may ask Claude for leads; record what you asked and what you verified in your AI-Use Log.
✅ BEFORE YOU LOG OFF

Check three things: the Quick Write is submitted, your project document has a Confucius section, and you have written a three-way comparison of the Gita, the Bible, and Confucius. Three traditions down, four to go.
